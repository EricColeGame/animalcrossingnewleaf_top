## Why Your First Ten Minutes Decide Your Look

The train ride into your new town is the only moment that shapes your mayor's face in *Animal Crossing: New Leaf*, and the game never warns you. Rover's small talk determines your face shape and your eye style, and once you step onto the platform, that look is locked to your save file. That is exactly why an **animal crossing new leaf appearance guide** is worth reading before you press start. This animal crossing new leaf appearance guide breaks down every question Rover asks, the answer combinations community charts have documented, and the parts of your look you can still tweak later.

Most players learn this the hard way. They answer Rover casually, play for forty hours, then notice their mayor's eyes are not what they pictured. By then, fixing it means deleting the whole town.

## How Character Creation Works in Animal Crossing: New Leaf

When you begin a brand-new save file, you board a train and sit across from Rover. He confirms your player name and your town name, then starts chatting about your trip. Three of those chat questions decide your character's face.

Community guide authors stress one thing: exact wording matters. Charts translated loosely from the Japanese release can push you toward a different face, because the English version phrases the answer options differently. A chart built on the exact English text removes the guesswork entirely, which is why long-running fan resources like the JVGS face guide make a point of quoting the in-game wording verbatim. For broader series context, Nintendo's official Animal Crossing hub is a solid reference point.

Here is how the three questions break down, based on community chart documentation:

| Order | What Rover is asking about | What your answer controls |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How familiar you are with the town | Which branch of the face chart you enter |
| 2 | Whether you are visiting or relocating | Which group of faces you land in |
| 3 | Whether you already have a place lined up | The exact face and eye style you receive |

The game itself never labels these questions this way. The structure comes from players who mapped hundreds of answer combinations and photographed the results.

## Answer Paths That Lead to Specific Faces

Because the face chart is a branching tree, the final answer is usually the one that flips you into a completely different face. Two paths appear again and again in community charts:

| Question 1 answer | Question 2 answer | Question 3 answer | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| "I've never been there." | "I'm moving." | "I'll get a place there." | Chart face A |
| "I've never been there." | "I'm moving." | "I'm sure I'll be fine." | Chart face B |

Notice how little separates those two paths. One phrase at the end of the conversation is the difference between two very different mayors.

A useful detail from player experience: most charts display **two** images per answer path, not one. The first image shows the resulting face, and the second shows the eye color that comes with it. Beginners often match the face, close the tab, and never check the second image, then wonder why their mayor's eyes look off.

One warning about copying charts blindly: different guides number faces differently. Always match the **picture**, not a number or a nickname someone invented on a forum.

## Eye Color and Eye Style Matter Just As Much

Your eyes are not a separate choice. According to community documentation, the same three answers that produce your face also determine your eye color, which is why dedicated eye color charts exist alongside face charts. If you want a specific combination, such as a particular face shape with green eyes rather than brown, you need to plan both at once.

This is the single most common regret reported by players: a face they love paired with an eye color they did not expect. Because the two are tied together, there is no way to mix and match after the fact in the base game.

| Appearance element | Decided by | Changeable later? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face shape | Rover's three questions | No | Permanently tied to the save file |
| Eye shape and color | The same three answers | No | Check a dedicated eye color chart |
| Hairstyle | Your starting choice, then Shampoodle | Yes | Harriet on Main Street |
| Hair color | Shampoodle | Yes | Can be recolored repeatedly |
| Outfit and accessories | Shopping | Yes | Able Sisters, Kicks, and other shops |
| Tan | Summer sun exposure | Yes, it fades | Hats and umbrellas reduce it |

That table is the heart of any animal crossing new leaf appearance guide. Hair, clothes, and accessories are all flexible. The face and eyes are not.

## What You Can Actually Change After You Start

Once you are settled in town, your customization options open up, but they are narrower than newcomers expect.

**Shampoodle** is the main one. Harriet's salon opens on Main Street after you have spent enough Bells at the Able Sisters shop and met the other unlock conditions. From then on, you can walk in and completely restyle your hair, including color, as often as you like. If you are chasing a specific look, Shampoodle covers most of it.

**Everything else is cosmetic and reversible.** Shirts, dresses, hats, glasses, shoes, and umbrellas can all be swapped freely through shops and the Able Sisters' design features.

**Your face is the exception.** There is no salon, no mirror, and no item that changes the face or eyes you were given on the train. The only route back is to delete the town and start a fresh save file.

Here is how that compares to the newest entry in the series, which many players arrive from:

| Feature | Animal Crossing: New Leaf | Animal Crossing: New Horizons |
|---|---|---|
| Face and eyes | Locked at creation | Changeable anytime at a mirror |
| Eye color | Locked at creation | Changeable anytime at a mirror |
| Hair | Shampoodle, after unlocking | Mirror, from day one |
| Cost of a redo | Delete the town and restart | Nothing at all |

That contrast is why so many returning players get caught out. Habits built in New Horizons do not transfer.

## How to Plan Your Look Before You Board the Train

A few minutes of preparation saves hours of regret. Work through this before starting a new save:

1. **Decide your target face first.** Browse a face chart and pick the image you want, not a description of it.
2. **Check the matching eye color.** Look at the second image in that chart row.
3. **Write the three answers down.** Keep them somewhere you can read while Rover is talking.
4. **Use the exact English wording.** Paraphrasing the options is how people end up on the wrong branch.
5. **Do not mash the A button.** Rover's dialogue moves quickly, but the answers are not reversible.
6. **Restart immediately if you slip.** The train ride takes only a few minutes, so a reroll is cheap.

Here is a quick way to prioritize, depending on what you care about most:

| Your goal | What to focus on at creation | Can it be fixed later? |
|---|---|---|
| A specific cute or cool face | The face chart | No, plan it now |
| A specific eye color | The eye color chart | No, plan it now |
| Trendy hair or a bold color | Nothing, relax | Yes, at Shampoodle |
| A seasonal, sun-kissed look | Nothing | Yes, through sun exposure |
| A themed outfit for your town | Nothing | Yes, through shopping |

Community reports suggest that players who write their answers down before starting almost never need a second attempt. Players who wing it often do.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you change your face in Animal Crossing: New Leaf after the game starts?**
No. Your face and eye color are locked to your save file the moment you finish talking to Rover. The only way to get a different face is to delete the town and begin a new save. This is the single biggest reason an animal crossing new leaf appearance guide is worth reading before your first train ride.

**How many questions does Rover actually ask?**
Three questions about your trip decide your appearance, on top of confirming your player name and town name. Each answer narrows the chart further, and the third answer is usually the one that determines the final face.

**Does eye color come from the same answers as the face shape?**
According to community-documented charts, yes. The same three answers produce both your face and your eye color, which is why most charts show two images per answer path.

**What if I only want to change my hair?**
That one is easy. Unlock Shampoodle on Main Street by spending Bells at the Able Sisters, then let Harriet restyle and recolor your hair as often as you like. Your face stays exactly as it was, which is usually fine, since hair is the part other players notice first anyway.
