## The Train Ride: Rover's Questions, Map Selection, and Face Shaping

Your journey begins aboard a train car opposite Rover, a blue-and-white cat whose friendly banter carries lasting consequences. In *Animal Crossing: New Leaf*, Rover's initial questionnaire determines your character's permanent facial appearance, eye shape, and starting expression. While you can change your hairstyle, eye color, and clothing later via Main Street shops, your core facial features cannot be altered.

```
       Rover's First Branching Choice
             /                \
   "I'm moving."          "Just traveling."
        |                         |
  [Male Path]                [Female Path]
```

According to community reports and dedicated player logs, your answers to questions like "Why are you traveling?" and "Do you have enough money?" trace a decision tree that locks in one of twelve distinct facial archetypes per gender.

Along with shaping your appearance, Rover presents four randomly generated town maps. Your town layout is permanent unless you reset your cartridge, so evaluate your choices carefully. Look for a central Town Hall, a conveniently situated Re-Tail shop near the beach ramps, and an unbroken stretch of land that allows ample space for future Public Works Projects (PWPs). Avoid layouts where the river divides your town into inconvenient slivers or isolates a private beach without ramp access.

| Map Element | Ideal Placement | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Re-Tail (Reese & Cyrus)** | Close to the beach ramp | Minimizes walking time when selling valuable beetles and fish. |
| **Town Hall** | Central or near northern cliff | Keeps administrative travel brief without blocking prime villager acres. |
| **River Shape** | Single gentle curve | Prevents awkward land fragments and makes bridge placement effortless. |
| **Beach Access** | Two accessible ramps | Eliminates the need for a wetsuit just to explore your entire shoreline. |
| **Town Plaza / Event Tree** | Open, non-constricted acre | Provides clear sightlines during seasonal holidays and special vendor visits. |

## Day One Priorities: Establishing Your Mayoral Office

Upon arrival, your animal neighbors will mistake you for the incoming mayor, escorting you straight to the Town Hall. There you will meet Isabelle, your devoted Shih Tzu secretary who will serve as your right hand throughout your tenure. 

Your immediate objective on Day One is securing a plot of land for your house. Visit Tom Nook at Nook's Homes on Main Street to select your building plot. Place your temporary tent in a scenic area with plenty of buffer space; once built, your house cannot be relocated. After pitching your tent, return to Tom Nook, who will inform you that your initial home construction requires a down payment of 10,000 Bells.

To raise these funds on your first afternoon, focus on natural resource gathering. Pick all native fruits from your trees, gather seashells along the shorelines, and sell everything to Reese at Re-Tail rather than the Nooklings, as Re-Tail pays 20% more for all items. Consult [IGN's comprehensive walkthrough of New Leaf](https://www.ign.com/wikis/animal-crossing-new-leaf/The_First_Day) for deeper step-by-step breakdowns of the introductory ceremonies and initial villager greetings.

| Day One Activity | Reward / Outcome | Priority Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pitching Tent with Tom Nook** | Establishes your permanent house location | Critical |
| **Town Plaza Tree Planting** | Officially inaugurates your mayorship | Mandatory |
| **Buying Shovel & Fishing Rod** | Enables daily resource harvesting | High |
| **Gathering Native Fruit & Shells** | Generates the 10,000 Bell house deposit | High |
| **Talking to Isabelle for Advice** | Rewards tools, foreign fruit, and watering can | High |

Be sure to speak repeatedly with Isabelle at the Town Hall counter. Selecting "Advice about living here" prompts her to offer tutorials. Completing her mini-tasks yields a fishing rod or bug net, your town permit requirements, and a basket of non-native foreign fruit that sells for higher prices when planted and harvested.

## Unlocking Town Hall Development: 100% Approval Rating

Before you can construct bridges, install decorative benches, or enact municipal ordinances, you must prove your dedication to your citizens by securing a 100% Town Approval Rating. Isabelle tracks this metric, which reflects your positive impact on the community.

Player experience shows that many new mayors find this milestone tedious if they do not know which actions yield the highest approval points. Fortunately, you can complete this requirement in a single afternoon by systematically checking off civic duties.

| Civic Action | Approval Points Earned | Daily Limit / Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Changing the Town Tune** | 3 Points | Once |
| **Changing the Town Flag** | 3 Points | Once |
| **Selling Items to Re-Tail** | 1 Point per transaction | Capped daily |
| **Pulling Town Weeds** | 1 Point per weed | Up to 16 points daily |
| **Watering Dried Flowers** | 1 Point per flower | Up to 14 points daily |
| **Posting a Bulletin Board Notice**| 3 Points | Once |
| **Completing Villager Favors** | 3 Points per task | Ongoing |

Once Isabelle congratulates you on hitting 100%, she will register your mayoral permit overnight. The following morning, sitting in the mayoral chair behind her desk unlocks the ability to propose Public Works Projects and pass town-wide ordinances.

```
       Mayor's Approval Roadmap
 [Arrive in Town] -> [Pitch Tent & Pay 10,000 Bells]
                           |
       [Complete Isabelle's Civic Tasks]
                           |
       [Reach 100% Citizen Approval Rating]
                           |
       [Next Morning: Ordinances & PWPs Unlocked]
```

Four distinct ordinances let you tailor the game to your daily schedule:

- **Beautiful Town:** Flowers never wilt, weeds rarely spawn, and trash cannot be fished from waters. This is widely considered the best ordinance for beginners and players who cannot log in every single day.
- **Early Bird:** Villagers wake up and shops open up to three hours earlier.
- **Night Owl:** Shops stay open up to three hours later, and villagers stay up past midnight.
- **Bell Boom:** Items sell for 20% more Bells at shops, though purchasing prices also rise by 20%.

## Building Your Fortune: Bell Making Strategies and Daily Routines

Accumulating wealth in *Animal Crossing: New Leaf* requires establishing a disciplined daily collection loop. Every morning at 6:00 AM, your town resets its hidden environmental assets. 

Begin every session by equipping your shovel. Strike every rock in your town from an angle. To prevent kickback recoil from canceling your combo, dig two holes directly behind your character before striking. One rock per day will spit out up to 16,100 Bells when struck eight consecutive times in rapid succession. Another rock is an impostor that shatters completely, dropping a valuable gem such as an amethyst, ruby, or gold nugget.

Furthermore, four fossils spawn buried in the ground each morning, marked by star-shaped cracks in the soil. Have Blathers at the Museum assess them. Early on, donate new specimens to build out the museum exhibits; sell duplicate fossils at Re-Tail for between 1,000 and 5,000 Bells each.

| Daily Asset | Daily Quantity | Estimated Bell Yield |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Money Rock** | 1 rock | 16,100 Bells |
| **Ore Rock** | 1 rock | 2,000 – 4,000 Bells (Gold sells for 4,000) |
| **Fossil Digs** | 4 spots | 6,000 – 18,000 Bells (if sold) |
| **Tree Shaking (Coins)** | 10 non-fruit trees | 1,000 Bells total |
| **Tree Furniture Drops** | 2 non-fruit trees | Free catalog items |

Following this **animal crossing new leaf beginner guide** routine will easily fund your initial room expansions. However, the true economic turning point occurs once you unlock Tortimer Island.

### The Ultimate Cash Cow: Tortimer Island Beetle Farming

A few days after paying off your second home loan, former mayor Tortimer will greet you at the dock. From then on, Kapp'n will ferry you to Tortimer Island for 1,000 Bells round-trip. The island exists in eternal summer, meaning rare tropical beetles spawn year-round.

To optimize your earnings, travel to the island after 7:00 PM. Strip the island's interior of regular trees, bushes, and flowers to prevent cheap bugs like fruit beetles and butterflies from occupying the spawn pool. Leave only the palm trees along the exterior beaches. 

Hold down the 'A' button to sneak forward with your net at creeping speed whenever you spot rare horned beetles clinging to the trunks. Fill your wicker transport basket with Golden Stags, Horned Hercules, and Goliath Beetles. A single full basket of 40 tropical beetles sells for upwards of 350,000 to 450,000 Bells back at Re-Tail, completely eliminating your mortgage worries.

## Public Works Projects and Main Street Expansion

Developing your town requires expanding the commercial district across the northern railroad tracks. Main Street starts with only the post office, Nook's Homes, the Museum, and a humble Nookling Junction shop. Spending money at Main Street vendors triggers upgrades that attract new shopkeepers.

| Shop / Expansion | Unlock Requirement | Key Benefits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T&T Convenience** | Spend 12,000 Bells at Nookling Junction + 10 days played | Longer hours, walls/floors, slingshots |
| **Kicks (Shoe Store)** | Spend 8,000 Bells at Able Sisters + 10 days played | Shoes, boots, and custom socks |
| **Shampoodle** | Spend 10,000 Bells at Able Sisters/Kicks + 7 days from Kicks unlock | Hairstyles, hair colors, eye contacts |
| **Club LOL** | Upgrade to T&T Mart + collect 6 villager petition signatures | K.K. Slider music, comedic emotions |
| **Dream Suite** | Spend 7 days as mayor + build at least 1 Public Works Project | Visit player towns worldwide via dreams |
| **Fortune Teller Shop** | Have Katrina tell fortunes 20 times in your town plaza | Permanent shop, daily luck modifiers |

When managing your Public Works Projects inside the town borders, space them out carefully. Villagers will periodically walk up to you with an excited chime and exclamation mark—known within the community as a "ping." These pings frequently suggest new PWP blueprints, ranging from useful functional installations like the Police Station and Reset Center to decorative wind turbines and zen gardens. As noted in any thorough **animal crossing new leaf beginner guide**, building the Police Station should be your top PWP priority, as Booker or Copper will maintain a lost-and-found bin that regularly yields rare free items.

## Mastering Everyday Etiquette: Flowers, Tools, and Villagers

Maintaining high town status requires careful environmental stewardship. Running in *New Leaf* causes grass wear over time, gradually creating dirt patches along heavily trafficked routes. Furthermore, sprinting through flower beds will destroy blossoms instantly. Walk when navigating through your residential zones and gardens.

Build your tool collection early. Keep your eye on the Nookling store racks daily to upgrade from standard equipment to Silver and Gold tools:

- **Silver Shovel:** Obtained from the Museum shop; sometimes causes the money rock to dispense high-value gems instead of Bells.
- **Silver Slingshot:** Floats across the sky attached to two silver balloons; shoots two pellets simultaneously.
- **Gold Watering Can:** Rewarded by Isabelle after maintaining a "Perfect Town" environmental rating for 15 consecutive days; waters a 3x3 grid of flowers simultaneously and turns wilted black roses into golden roses.

Keep your favorite neighbors happy by mailing them letters with attached gifts, responding warmly to their requests, and visiting their homes when invited. A strong friendship eventually rewards you with the villager's framed photograph, the ultimate badge of neighborly adoration in *New Leaf*.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do first in this animal crossing new leaf beginner guide?
Start by choosing a favorable town map with Rover, placing your tent near a convenient path, and speaking with Isabelle at Town Hall. Gather shells and native fruit to pay off your initial 10,000 Bell down payment to Tom Nook, then focus on Isabelle's tasks to earn your initial tools and work toward a 100% Town Approval Rating.

### How do I stop villagers from moving away unexpectedly?
Villagers will alert you before moving. If you speak with them regularly, a departing neighbor will eventually approach you with an exclamation ping to announce their moving date. You can simply ask them to stay, and they will cancel their plans. If you take a long break from playing, save and quit immediately on a day when a neighbor has just asked to move and you convinced them to remain; the game will not initiate a new moving queue while you are inactive.

### Which town ordinance is best for a beginner?
The **Beautiful Town** ordinance is overwhelmingly recommended for new mayors. It eliminates the frustration of wilting flowers, stops weeds from overtaking your paths, and removes trash from rivers and oceans. This lets you explore the game at your own pace without logging in every 24 hours just to water plants.

### How do I get foreign fruits in Animal Crossing: New Leaf?
You can acquire your first non-native fruit by completing Isabelle's optional living advice tutorials on Day One. Additional exotic fruits—such as mangos, lychees, durians, lemons, and coconuts—can be gathered during Tortimer Island minigames (tours). Pick fruit from the trees during these excursions, store it in your pockets, and plant it in your home town soil using a shovel.
