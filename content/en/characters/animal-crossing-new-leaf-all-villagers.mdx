## How Many Villagers Are in Animal Crossing New Leaf?

The base game ships with **333 animal villagers**. That number does not include special characters like Isabelle, Tom Nook, K.K. Slider, or the Able sisters — those NPCs live in your town but never move in, never ask for fruit, and never hand over a framed photo.

Community tallies of the Welcome amiibo update put the added villagers at roughly **50 more**, which pushes the total roster past 380. Because a town holds a maximum of ten residents, you'll realistically meet only a small slice of that lineup in a single save file.

| Roster Detail | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Animal villagers in base game | 333 | Excludes special characters |
| Villagers added in Welcome amiibo | ~50 | Community count; amiibo cards required |
| Maximum residents in your town | 10 | Plus your player character |
| Species represented | 30+ | Deer and hamsters debut in New Leaf |
| Personality types | 8 | Smug and Uchi debut in New Leaf |
| Share of roster you can host | ~3% | 10 out of 380+ villagers |

Nookipedia's roster breakdown traces 145 of those villagers back to the original Doubutsu no Mori, 11 to the first Animal Crossing, and 42 to Doubutsu no Mori e+ — the rest were created or redesigned for New Leaf itself. ## How to Get Specific Villagers in Your Town

The Animal Crossing New Leaf all villagers list tells you who exists — this section covers how to actually get them. There are several routes, and some are dramatically faster than others.

| Method | How It Works | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Campsite | Build the campsite PWP; a random camper appears and can be invited if you have space | Random surprises |
| amiibo cards | Scan a villager card using Wisp to invite that villager directly | Guaranteed dreamies |
| RV amiibo cards | Brings a camper in an RV with exclusive furniture | Rare items plus the villager |
| Trading with friends | Visit a town where a villager is packed up and ask them to move in | Specific, hard-to-find villagers |
| StreetPass | Villagers from other towns can move in through StreetPass | Passive collecting |
| Natural move-ins | A new plot appears every few days when you have an open slot | Filling empty space |

A few practical pointers that veteran players swear by:

- **Keep one slot open.** With nine villagers or fewer, campers and amiibo invites can actually move in. At ten, they'll turn you down.
- **Talk to villagers on their last day.** A resident "in boxes" can be invited to your town during that single day — miss it and they're gone.
- **Mind the 16-villager cycle.** Community-documented testing says a villager who moved out can't move back in until 16 other villagers have cycled through your town.
- **Watch the void.** Villagers who leave without being adopted may relocate to a friend's town if you've visited each other recently.
- **Check plot placement before saving.** Many players quit without saving on move-in day to reroll an ugly plot location — a well-known community technique.

For most of the Animal Crossing New Leaf all villagers lineup, amiibo cards are optional. The base 333 residents can all appear naturally; the added Welcome amiibo villagers are the ones that require a card scan.

## FAQ

**How many villagers are in Animal Crossing New Leaf in total?**
The base game includes 333 animal villagers, not counting special characters. The Welcome amiibo update added roughly 50 more according to community counts, bringing the total past 380.

**Can you get every villager in one town?**
No. A town holds a maximum of ten residents, so you'll only ever house a small fraction of the Animal Crossing New Leaf all villagers total at once. Long-term players cycle residents in and out over multiple years.

**Which species and personalities were introduced in New Leaf?**
Deer and hamsters debuted as new species, and Smug and Uchi joined as new personality types. Both additions are a big reason the New Leaf roster feels so much deeper than earlier entries.

**Do I need amiibo cards to complete the roster?**
Only for the Welcome amiibo additions. Every villager from the original 333 can move in through normal gameplay, the campsite, or trading with other players.

**Is a popular villager better than an unpopular one?**
Not in gameplay terms. Popularity only affects trade demand within the community. Any villager can become a best friend, give you their picture, and suggest public works projects — the "best" villager is simply the one you enjoy talking to every day.
