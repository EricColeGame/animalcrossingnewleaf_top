## Why Your First Five Residents Shape Your Whole Town

Five faces are waiting when you step off the train, and you never got to choose a single one of them. That is the reality of Animal Crossing New Leaf starting villagers: the game rolls them before you even name your town, and the only way to change the outcome is to start over. It matters more than most mayors expect, because your opening residents set the tone for daily chats, requests, public works projects, and the slow, cozy rhythm of your first year in office. Understanding how Animal Crossing New Leaf starting villagers are generated — and how much control you really have — can save you from months of quiet regret.

## How the Game Picks Your Animal Crossing New Leaf Starting Villagers

Town creation happens in one burst of randomness. Before you ever meet Isabelle at Town Hall, the game has already decided your map layout, your native fruit, your character's face, and the five animals who will greet you. Rover's questions on the train influence your face; nearly everything else is a dice roll you never see.

Your town begins with five villagers, each with a house already planted somewhere on the map. Within the first few days, a sixth resident moves in to fill an open plot, and the population can eventually climb to ten. Because villager houses occupy real estate, your opening lineup also shapes how much room you have for paths, orchards, and future projects.

| Element | Locked in at creation? | Can you change it later? |
|---|---|---|
| Town map and river | Yes | Only by deleting the town |
| Native fruit | Yes | Only by deleting the town |
| Character face | Yes (set by Rover's questions) | Hair yes via Shampoodle, face no |
| Your five starting villagers | Yes | Only by letting them move out |
| Villager house plots | Yes | Frees up when a villager leaves |
| Sixth move-in | No | Arrives within the first few days |

Community reports suggest the opening five are drawn from the standard villager pool with a few quirks. The newcomers tied to the Welcome amiibo update rarely if ever appear as starters — most mayors invite them with a card instead. Beyond that, the roll is random, which is exactly why so many players restart their town on day one.

| Town population | What typically happens |
|---|---|
| 5 residents | Your fixed starting lineup |
| 6 residents | First move-in arrives within days |
| 7–9 residents | Random move-ins possible from the void or StreetPass |
| 10 residents | Town full — swaps only via campsite or amiibo |

## The Eight Personalities Behind Your Opening Lineup

Animal Crossing New Leaf introduced two brand-new personality types — Smug and Uchi, also called Sisterly — bringing the total to eight. Each personality has its own dialogue pool, hobbies, and request style, so a town with a healthy spread feels far livelier than one dominated by a single type.

| Personality | Vibe | Typical requests | Popular examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Sweet, bookish, motherly | Errands, gardening | Marina, Fauna, Lolly, Molly |
| Peppy | Bubbly, aspiring pop star | Fashion, gossip | Rosie, Tangy, Bunnie, Chrissy |
| Snooty | Elegant, blunt, stylish | Furniture, clothing | Ankha, Diana, Whitney, Olivia |
| Lazy | Relaxed, snack-obsessed | Food, bugs, naps | Bob, Stitches, Beau, Punchy |
| Jock | Fitness-obsessed | Sports gear, workouts | Kid Cat, Rudy, Genji, Hamlet |
| Cranky | Gruff with a soft center | Old-school items | Fang, Chief, Apollo, Kabuki |
| Smug | Flirty, self-assured | Coffee, fancy furniture | Marshal, Julian, Kyle, Zell |
| Uchi | Tough big-sister energy | Advice, town projects | Phoebe, Muffy, Cherry, Fuchsia |

Personality also decides which public works projects villagers suggest. If you are hoping for a specific bridge, fountain, or sculpture, the quickest route is keeping at least one resident of the personality that pitches it. That is one more reason your opening five deserve a second look before you commit to a permanent town.

Friendship works the same way regardless of personality. Talking daily, running small errands, and delivering packages all raise your bond, and higher friendship unlocks deeper conversations, nicknames, and the chance to buy items straight out of a villager's house.

## Can You Choose or Reroll Your Animal Crossing New Leaf Starting Villagers?

Short answer: you cannot pick them, but you can reroll them. Animal Crossing New Leaf starting villagers are assigned by the same random roll as your map, so the two are tangled together — reset for a better lineup and you may lose a map you loved.

The reroll window closes the moment the game saves your new town. Before that first save, quitting the software and relaunching gives you a fresh roll in just a few minutes. After the save, your only option is deleting the town from the title screen, where Isabelle offers to re-create it — a much bigger commitment.

| Method | How it works | Effort | What you risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quit before the first save | Close the software during the intro, relaunch | Low | A few minutes |
| Re-create the town | Delete the save from the title screen | High | Every item, bell, and badge |
| Amiibo card invite | Scan a card at Wisp and swap out a resident | Low | Needs cards and a full town |
| Campsite invite | Build the campsite, then recruit a camper | Medium | Camper is random |
| Wait for a move-out | Agree when a villager pings to leave | Very slow | Weeks of waiting |

Player experience says a serious "dreamie hunt" can take dozens of resets, and some mayors report hundreds. If you go that route, decide in advance which two or three villagers you would happily accept, then stop the moment one appears — otherwise you can burn an entire weekend chasing a perfect roll that never lands.

## Getting the Animal Crossing New Leaf Starting Villagers You Want Later

You do not have to restart to fix a lineup you dislike. Animal Crossing New Leaf hands you several slower, gentler tools:

- **The campsite.** Build it as a public works project and a random camper will appear regularly. Win their game and they may agree to move in.
- **Amiibo cards.** With the Welcome amiibo update, Wisp lets you invite a specific villager from a card. If your town is full, you choose who moves out — the cleanest way to replace a starter you never clicked with.
- **Adoption from a friend's town.** When a villager is packed up and ready to leave, a visiting player can talk to them and invite them home.
- **The void.** Villagers who leave a town you have visited can drift into yours, which is why surprise move-ins sometimes look oddly familiar.
- **StreetPass and Main Street.** Residents who moved away often reappear shopping on Main Street, and StreetPass data shuffles villagers between nearby towns.

Many mayors also keep their population at nine rather than ten, since a town with an open slot can receive a random newcomer from the void. It is not a guarantee, but it is the strategy most often recommended by the community for keeping a curated lineup intact.

## Tips for Living With a Lineup You Did Not Choose

Not every mayor wants to reset. If you decide to keep your Animal Crossing New Leaf starting villagers, a few habits make the early game much smoother:

| Tip | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Talk to everyone daily | Builds friendship and unlocks more requests |
| Complete small errands | Friendship grows faster than with gifts alone |
| Keep one of each personality | More dialogue variety and project suggestions |
| Do not ignore a villager you dislike | Ignoring does not speed up move-outs; the ping is random |
| Say yes to move-out pings | A departing villager opens a plot for someone new |

Villagers in New Leaf are persistent. They will not leave because you avoid them, and they will not stay because you adore them — the move-out prompt is a random event that eventually reaches everyone. If a starter is not working out, patience plus an amiibo card is usually faster than hoping they pack their bags.

## FAQ

**How many starting villagers do you get in Animal Crossing New Leaf?**
Your town begins with five villagers, each already settled into a house. A sixth resident moves in within the first few days, and the population can grow to ten over time.

**Can you choose your Animal Crossing New Leaf starting villagers?**
No. The opening five are generated randomly when the town is created, along with your map, fruit, and character face. Your only direct control is rerolling before the town saves, or deleting and re-creating it later.

**Is there a "best" Animal Crossing New Leaf starting villagers lineup?**
There is no mechanical advantage to any lineup — every villager can move out and be replaced. Pick based on personalities you enjoy talking to, and try to cover several types so your town feels varied.

**Do starting villagers ever move away?**
Yes. Any villager can eventually ping you about moving, including the ones who were there on day one. If you want a specific animal gone sooner, an amiibo card lets you invite a new resident and choose who leaves.
